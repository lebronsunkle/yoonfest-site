FAQ

Tickets

When and where is YOON Fest 2027?
May 7 to 9, 2027, in the Sequoia Forest near Kings Canyon, California. Three nights of house, techno and electro, in collaboration with RAW CUTS.
Where do I buy tickets?
Tickets are sold through Shotgun. Sign up there to get notified when each tier drops.
Are payment plans available?
Yes, payment plans are offered on select ticket tiers through Shotgun.
Is there an age limit?
YOON Fest is 21+. Bring a valid government photo ID. No one under 21 will be admitted.

Getting there

How do I get there?
The festival is in the mountains east of Fresno, off Highway 180. Plan on about an hour from Fresno and roughly 4 to 5 hours from Los Angeles or the Bay Area. The last stretch is a dirt road, so take it slow.
Where exactly is it?
The exact address and directions go out to ticket holders before the festival.
Where should I get gas?
Fill up before you head up the mountain. The last gas station is at Clingans Junction on Highway 180.
Do I need a vehicle pass?
Yes. Every vehicle that enters needs a pass: a car pass for cars, or an RV / trailer pass for RVs, vans and trailers. Get them on Shotgun. Carpooling saves you money and parking space.
Is there a size limit for RVs and trailers?
Yes. RVs and trailers can be up to 30 feet long. If yours is longer, contact us before buying a pass.

Camping and lodging

Is camping included?
Yes. Most of YOON camps under the trees. Bring your own tent and gear.
Can I stay somewhere with a bed?
A limited number of yurts and one private cabin are available for the festival weekend. See Lodging for photos and details. All lodging sales are final and non-refundable.
Are there showers and bathrooms?
Yes. The bath house has a men's side and a women's side, each with 4 hot showers plus toilets and sinks. There will also be porta potties in the camping area.

Food and drink

Will there be food?
Yes. There will be food vendors on site, and you are welcome to bring your own food to camp.
Is there a bar?
Yes, there is a bar at the festival.
Can I bring my own alcohol?
For drinking at your camp only. Each car is limited to one 30 rack of beer plus one plastic handle of liquor. No outside drinks are allowed in the stage area.

Rules

Can I bring my dog?
No pets. Only approved service animals are allowed.
Can I bring my own speakers or decks?
No renegade sound systems or parties of any kind. Speakers, sound systems and decks will be taken by security.

What to bring

What should I pack?
Tent, sleeping bag and pad, warm layers (mountain nights get cold in May), a headlamp, a refillable water bottle, sunscreen, your ID and your tickets. Bring your food and camp drinks within the limits above.
